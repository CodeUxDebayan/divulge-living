Chapter I · One season of jute, from silt to strand

The Strand

01

Sown in silt

As the pre-monsoon rains arrive, jute is sown across the alluvial floodplains of the Ganges–Brahmaputra delta. In Bengal it is simply pāṭ.

02

A hundred and twenty days

CO₂ drawn down, per hectare
15t
Oxygen released
11t

Figures as widely cited for one growing season; its fallen leaves return to the field as green manure.

03

Retting

Cut stalks are sunk in still village ponds for two to three weeks. Native microbes loosen the bark. No solvent, no furnace — only water, time and patience.

পাট

04

The golden strand

Stripped by hand, washed and sun-dried, the fibre takes on its lustre: cellulose and lignin, grown rather than refined.

05

Braided & crocheted by hand

Three plies, two hands, unbroken tension — the foundation for our signature crochet bags, structured totes, and sculpted home décor accents.

06

And nothing wasted

Every offcut is twisted back into twine, trims and tassels. In true zero-waste ecology, our agricultural cycle leaves zero residue: the tender, antioxidant-rich leaves of the jute plant become Amata Moroheiya Infusion — a nourishing botanical tea. From leaf to stalk to finished crochet bag, nothing is discarded.

Chapter III · How We Create · Howrah & Hooghly

The Hands

63++

Master Custodians Across Howrah & Hooghly · Still Growing

Rural women across the courtyards of Howrah and Hooghly form the living heartbeat of our collective. Not anonymous labour — but a thriving sisterhood of master artisans crafting export-grade luxury with financial sovereignty and creative pride.

For decades, rural craftswomen were treated as silent hands at the end of exploitative middlemen chains. Divulge was born to flip that reality on its head. We began around a single village veranda in Howrah and expanded across Hooghly; today, over 63 master craftswomen — and an ever-expanding circle — earn dignified fair-living wages, work flexibly from their ancestral homes, and pass on generational wisdom with pride.

Every bag carries the warmth of afternoon laughter, shared Bengali songs, and export-calibrated crochet precision. Here, when a woman picks up the crochet needle, she doesn't just craft sustainable luxury — she opens her first independent savings account, funds her daughters’ schooling, and commands honor in her household. Every stitch proves our promise: Every weave has a story.

Trade, Sourcing & Craft Answers

Frequently Asked Export & Sourcing Questions

Why is Divulge Living recognized as a leading handmade jute product exporter from India?

Divulge Living unites direct artisan cluster manufacturing across Howrah and Hooghly, West Bengal (63++ master women artisans) with verified global export licenses — including DGFT IEC (AUJPG7653C), National Jute Board JDP registration, and JCI empanelment. Following our landmark first international expo at Messe Berlin (Stand 1B-37) and over 275,000+ creations carried globally, we offer zero-middleman export pricing, boutique-to-bulk scalability, and 100% plastic-free biodegradable craft.

Does Divulge Living accept wholesale, bulk, and custom OEM private-label orders?

Yes. We manufacture custom OEM and private-label crochet totes, juco bags, promotional shopping bags, corporate gift kits, and braided home décor. Buyers can tailor bag dimensions, gussets, GSM weight, handles (braided jute, cotton webbing, saddle leather, cane), AZO-free natural dyeing, embossed wooden or leather tags, custom metal logo pins, and eco-packaging.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ), sampling policy, and global shipping timeline?

We support agile capsule wholesale orders starting from an MOQ of 100 pieces per design as well as container-scale export volumes. Custom prototype samples are dispatched worldwide prior to production, with a guaranteed 24-hour export quote turnaround via info@divulge-living.com and FOB Kolkata / CIF air and sea freight worldwide.

What government certifications and international eco-standards does Divulge hold?

Divulge is DGFT Export Certified (IEC AUJPG7653C), registered with the National Jute Board (NJB-09-JDP-20022025-951), empanelled by the Jute Corporation of India (JCI), MSME Udyam certified (UDYAM-WB-10-0013953), GST verified (19AUJPG7653C1ZJ), and Trademark registered (No. 2909685). All crafts use AZO-free dyes and zero synthetic plastic stiffeners.

Where are Divulge Living’s artisan workshops and export hub located?

Our rural crocheting and braiding clusters operate across Amraberia, Howrah and Hooghly, West Bengal — the historic heartland of Bengal’s golden jute belt — supported by commercial export dispatch from Kolkata, India.

How does Divulge’s zero-waste process and 45-day soil return work?

Every cutting remnant is hand-twisted into trims and tassels, while sister venture Amata harvests the plant’s tender leaves for organic Moroheiya prebiotic herbal infusions. Free of petrochemical coatings, untreated jute dissolves back into fertile soil humus in around 45 days.
