# Return & Refund Policy

**Brand:** DIVULGE Handicrafts  
**Category:** Company Policy  
**Status:** Official Active Policy  

---


### Requirements of a Return:

- The return from the customer/ Consumer has to be initiated within 4 days of receiving the product/ products.
- The return must be done in the original packaging used by our company.
- The return can be done in two ways (a) Full return of the money (b) In store credit, which can be used in future shopping with us.
- One “Order Number”(mentioned in the purchase receipt) will have only one return service provided. Repeated returns from the same “Order number” will not be entertained.

### Full return of the money:

To avail this service you need to

- The return from the customer/ Consumer has to be initiated within 4 days of receiving the product.
- The return must be done in the original packaging used by our company.
- Take the picture of the product and upload to our website under “Return” tab through your order section together with the purchase receipt.
- The return of money will be initiated after receiving the product in our ware house and comparing the same with your uploaded image.
- One letter of authorisation from the buyer has to be sent with the return package.
- The original purchase receipt needs to be attached.
- The return will be done on the same bank account by which the product was purchased.(Debit card, UPI, Wallet etc)
- The return of money may take 15 to 20 days depending on the situation.
- Bulk and customised orders will not be taken back.
- Used products will not be taken back.
- The transportation charges will be beard by the company.

### In store credit: (Recommended)

To avail this service you need to

- The return from the customer/ Consumer has to be initiated within 4 days of receiving the product.
- The return must be done in the original packaging used by our company.
- Take the picture of the product and upload to our website under “Return” tab through your order section together with the purchase receipt.
- The credit of money will be initiated after receiving the product in our ware house and comparing the same with your uploaded image.
- The credit of money will be done immediately to your account for future shopping. You can use it anytime you need to.
- Bulk and customised orders will not be taken back.
