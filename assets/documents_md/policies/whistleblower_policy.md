# Whistleblower Policy

**Brand:** DIVULGE Handicrafts  
**Category:** Company Policy  
**Status:** Official Active Policy  

---

“Divulge” is bound to high standards of environmental compliance by itself, its subordinates, and by all entities and covered personnel with whom it engages in business. To facilitate the open communication and effective implication of the “Environment Policy” the company decided to adopt a “whistleblower policy” to provide a doorway for employees, directors, contractors, subcontractors, and other covered personnel to raise concerns without fear of retaliation for reports made in good faith.


### This Policy shall encompass:

- The notice, detention, and treatment of complaints, whether or not in anonymous form, received by the Company relating to companies environment related operational matters.
- The notice, detention, and treatment of complaints, whether or not in anonymous form, received from any third party related to our scope of business even customers and consumers.

### Submission of complaints:

Any concerns related to our ethics and policies will be entertained in an unbiased way.

The name of the complainer will be kept hidden if they feel it necessary.

The initial complain has to be done to the immediate supervisor / point of contact available. 

If the complainer feels that the solution is not satisfactory then they can place their concern to   

The Chief Operating Officer of the Company (the “COO”) is designated by the committee as the point of contact for all concerns submitted via .

The company will try to solve the concern as soon as possible but subject to the seriousness and requirement of the complain. 

The Chief Operating Officer of the Company (the “COO”) is responsible to forward all the status of the complain (solved and unsolved) to the monthly committee meetings.

If COO feels necessary on such specific Complaints that may require the immediate attention of the Committee, the COO shall immediately contact the Founder or Co-Founder of the Company or else can demand for a immediate committee meeting.

The minimum retention is 3 years from the date of register.  

The complain must follow the “Chain of command”; otherwise it will not be registered as valid.
