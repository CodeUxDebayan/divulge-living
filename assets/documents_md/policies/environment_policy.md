# Environmental Policy

**Brand:** DIVULGE Handicrafts  
**Category:** Company Policy  
**Status:** Official Active Policy  

---

- This policy applies to all people as well as establishments which are directly or partially engaged in trade with “Divulge” except the users, customers and consumers.
- The company is liable to comply with all local government’s environmental jurisdictions and policies and will never interfere with their requirements.
- Company imposes ZERO TOLERANCE in intentional environmental violation and will take strict action against the performer (Applicable to in-house members and employees).
- Accidental violations will be scrutinised thoroughly followed by investigations and during that tenure the accused (Persons or establishment) will be taken out of service temporarily.
- This policy doesn’t relief any person or establishment of their responsibility towards keeping the environment clean in lieu the local government regulations.
- The Committee shall review the Policy annually and may amend it at any time, consistent with requirements of applicable laws, rules and regulations.
