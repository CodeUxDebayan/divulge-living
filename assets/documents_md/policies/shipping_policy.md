# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Brand:** DIVULGE Handicrafts  
**Category:** Company Policy  
**Status:** Official Active Policy  

---


### Shipping Policy:

- Divulge is taking great care on delivering the products to it’s customers through the well reputed courier agencies completely free of cost all over India.
- Shipping time may vary between 7 to 10 days depending on the delivery destination as well as any breakers on transportation scenario subject to the factors beyond our control (strikes, weather condition, lock down etc). Look for the shipping time information at the product page by applying PIN code of the destination.
- Customers can track the status of their product through the link which will be sent to them after the product is shipped.
- Address change will only be accepted till the product is shipped and is within the delivery service area of our delivery partner (Confirm that at the product page).

### Cancellations:

Cancellation will only be accepted within 24 hours of placing the order.
