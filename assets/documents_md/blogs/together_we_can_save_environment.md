Title: **T****ogether we can save environment for future generation**

As a consumer we possess an enormous ability in saving our environment by reflecting what we choose to use. A small change of habit can play a huge part in conservation of our future requirements. Each one of us, being a manufacturer, customer or a consumer has our own devoir towards the environment which comforts us with resources we need. That is why Divulge believes “together we can save environment for future generation and endeavours”. 

Think! What challenging platform we are gifting to our future generation, and act immediately:

We are facing a grave challenge of saving our home, our nature from various camouflages. Few are coming up as social, few as economical and a few holding the hands of habit. But all the related factors can be controlled very easily if we work together. Each individual has their part to play in this crisis. Thus we preach little propaganda for a better future

Go Green, Go clean, save environment:  Green as a colour represents the true self of our clean and flourished nature. We must remember that nature is our home, which provides us with all the commodities we entail to proceed with our life. So to save the green we must stop pollutions because it is the foundation of the natural destruction. We must accelerate the transformation towards sustainability and change of lifestyle. Only this can break the chain of deforestation, global warming, and excessive waste generation which are destroying the living atmosphere.  

Easy ways to go green: Earlier we were talking about the problem we are incorporating as well as the reasons behind that. Now its time to talk about the solutions that we start off with. As we mentioned earlier that we can control all the problems easily if we work together as one team. We must put Planet over profit and work for the greener future as well. For this the following can work as a solution

- Reduce waste
- Reuse commodity
- Recycle products
- Accept sustainability
- Buy sensible
- Get serious on Ecological Economics and think for the long run
- Reduce buying disposable goods as it destroys the natural resource
Jute fabrics can work as a substitute of all abovementioned solutions; we just need to apply it.

Awareness of climate change: Climate has a tendency to maintain an equilibrium state and for this she reforms as and when she requires. She always selects, one of the examples is **“the natural selection”.**  This change can be very perilous to us as in the process of getting back the stability the nature will destroy anything that may come to her path. We all know what an earthquake, tsunami and a high level hurricane can do.

It is our utmost responsibility to promote awareness on climate change, and at the same time show them the way by accepting it ourselves. To accelerate we must axis on the following 

- We should think about the global solutions rather inclosing to ourselves.
- Help others to reach the sate of environmental preservation by being an example
- Create a Go green culture in day to day life activities.
- Educate others of the devastating effects of the climate change.
- Repeated reminders in social media of the same.
Increased awareness can help in effectuating climate change adaption. A balance between man- made system and ecosystem can restrict the perils of climate change.

Examples of Climate change that can bring awareness:  The facts that can help in getting climate change awareness in practice are 

1. Respect towards nature, 

2. Our requirements of daily needs from nature and 

3.  Fear of nature. 

To know the importance of keeping our relation with nature stable we must know how the change can affect us and for that we must reminisce about the examples of it. The most common but neglected clues that our mother nature is continuously warning us with are 

- Increased heat
- Outbreaks of Insects
- Reduced fertility of the earth
- Reduced underground water supplies
- Dried out rivers
- Flooding
- Erosion in near coastal area
- Outbreaks of new illnesses
- Huge and frequent earthquakes
- Tsunamis
- Increased drought
- Increased power level of cyclones
- Animal species extinction
- Plant extinction
- Changing of underwater current as salinity of oceans are changing due to polar ice meltdown and many more.
The fact of fear is that most of these examples are in front of us but we choose to ignore thinking that it will not affect us. On the other hand we are lucky to have a class very conscious people too who through some different ways and tactics are trying to spread the message globally. As an example the movies like “The day after tomorrow”, “2012”, “An Inconvenient truth” etc reminds us of where we are steaming towards.
