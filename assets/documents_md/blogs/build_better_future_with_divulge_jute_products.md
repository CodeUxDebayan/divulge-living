### Go green jute products – an online venture

Jute is quintessentially one of the most environmental friendly giftour nature has provided us. With innumerable attributes, jute definitely follows all the environmental protection acts. The acts are initiated by the Government to establish a greener nature. We as a company aim to follow all the waste management norms, environmental non-degradability norms laid by the government.

Divulge offers a good range of Go green jute products and even plans to export in near future. We aspire to bring about recyclable products at your door step. Furthermore, jute products are proven to be biodegradable as well as eco-friendly. Likewise if we could make this products day to day household alternative, it will reduce risks of plastic pollution in the long run:


### Jute Bags:

Our online venture on jute bags offer a wide range of hand-made and machine-made bags for various purposes. Bags made of jute can be as safer replacement for polythene bags. Bags made of polythene are now a days recyclable but with limitations. Jute bags are fully recyclable and easily biodegradable. Thus is both easy to use and easy on the environment. In addition to this the bags are easy in maintenance, resilient, environmental friendly and fashionable. And never out of season. These bags come in all sizes and shapes.

### Jute Rugs, Jute Curtains, Table cover & runners:

Jute production is mainly based in West Bengal. Kolkata is the designing hub of jute product manufacturers. Rugs are one of the most beautiful artisans made from different quality of jute fibers. These rugs beautifies our house especially the living rooms and balconies. Curtains made of jute are not only ethnic in home decor but also heat resistant and UV ray resistant. Moreover these rugs, curtains and table cloth and runners are recyclable, eco-friendly and mostly hand made. Jute curtains, rugs, table cloths and table-runners are sustainable and easy to manufacture. Online services and manufacturing of jute products have a huge platform. Divulge aims to cultivate these opportunities as well as bring these beautiful home decors at your door step.
