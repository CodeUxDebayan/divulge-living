# Our Principles, Goals and Views

**Brand:** DIVULGE Handicrafts  
**Document Type:** Brand Story & Overview  

---


### Our Principles:

- We believe in business with strong ethical standards. Ethics, honesty, respect and strong moral principles are the four pillars of our establishment.
- We trust that disciplined dealings are the fundamentals for an organisation to grow and meet the expectations of our customers, consumers and most importantly the employees who gives us our existence.
- We do not compromise with our promises and provide the best possible by us in all aspect.
- We always work for the balanced long term relationships. We respect our customers, partners, peers and competitors as we are all in this together.
- We are always open to any changes suggested by anybody for the benefits of the environment, society as well as for the company itself without compromising our ethical boundaries as we believe “Only change is constant”

### Our Goals:

- Our main goal is to make it a peoples company where freedom of speech, expression and free flow of ideas will be no bar rather appreciated.
- We are steaming towards a pollution free environment with zero tolerance against the intentional violations.
- We will have one part which will be beneficial for the people in need in the rural areas.
- We will provide support to the people and architects who don’t get proper infrastructure and platform, by providing the same.
- We will strongly promote ‘Go green’ culture and Woman empowerment initiative.
- We will put our full effort in protecting our environment and sustain its natural resources for current and future generations because they deserve this.
- Our sole initiative is to promote a conscious life style and raise environmental awareness that will help the nature to regain her emergence.
- We will try to implement the “recycle culture” as a day to day practice by promoting both financial and environmental advantages.
