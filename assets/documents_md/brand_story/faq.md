### About the product

- What is Jute?
- “Jute” is the name of the plant or fibre used for making burlap, hessian or gunny cloth. It is a completely natural fibre with a diversifying usage. Various fabrics can be made by different processing of this fibre.
- Is jute financially beneficial?
- In long run absolutely beneficial. Please refer to our blogs for an explained view. Link is given at the bottom of the website home page.
- Is jute biodegradable?
- Completely biodegradable.
- Is jute recyclable?
- Absolutely.
- Is jute comfortable to wear in summers?
- Jute fabric provides better thermal conduction in comparison to any other natural fabric that does not let you perspire and makes the fabric cool and comfortable. Guys, it is the best fabric for summer.
- Advantages of jute fibres and fabrics?
- Jute fibre is absolutely bio-degradable, recyclable and environmental friendly.
- The fabric made of jute is strong as it has high tensile strength.
- The fabrics] made of jute have low extensibility.
- The fabric made of jute provides better breathability.
- The fabric made of jute has a good insulating and antistatic properties
- The fabric made of jute has low thermal conductivity and moderate moisture regaining property
- The fabric made of jute absorbs moisture to a certain level thus provides a cooling effect which will provide comfort indoors and is good for electrical appliances too.
- Disadvantages of jute fabrics?
- Crease resistance of jute fibre is very low; though blending with other biodegradable fibre resolves the matter completely.
- Initial costing may look higher than plastic.
- Raw jute fibre can cause skin irritation though various processing methods resolve this problem completely.
- Wet jute looses strength like other natural fibres.
- Does jute last well? How many days?
- Totally depends on the product, usage and the processing done on raw jute fibre.
