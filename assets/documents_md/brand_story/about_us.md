### About Us:

Divulge dreams of a world that flourishes, sustains and becomes greener day by day. We are a company that is honour bound to preserve the chastity of our environment. Our products will reflect every aspect of bio-preservation like biodegradability, recyclability, sustainability and resilience.

Albeit all the beneficial attributes, jute straggles in comparison to its contemporaries by factors such as availability, cost of manufacture and skilled man power. We will put all our efforts to accumulate these holdings together and present you with the upshot that qualifies as pre-eminent.

Despite having plethora of knowledge, our jute cultivators and artisans lack recognition. We will provide them the platform of appreciation and acknowledgement.

We are Divulge and we are the future.
